ABOUT PROLOGUE

Where stories become conversations.

PROLOGUE is the official reading club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women.

We believe that books have the power to inspire, challenge and connect people. PROLOGUE brings together students who enjoy reading, discussing ideas and discovering new perspectives.

Through literary activities, reading sessions, workshops and discussions, we create a welcoming space for every curious mind.
